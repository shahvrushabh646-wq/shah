# CMAT 2026 Hinglish Guide Coverage Map

This project combines five Hinglish study-guide volumes with the existing 35-module Mitra AI Tutor course. This map connects the topic groups in the student-supplied `CMAT_2026_Detailed_Expanded_Syllabus.pdf` to the in-app lessons. Module IDs are the stable IDs in `src/data/lessons.ts`.

The supplied PDF counts **119 checklist points in 21 QT/DI groups, 69 in 15 LR groups, 55 in 12 Language groups, 68 in 13 GA groups, and 31 in 6 I&E groups**. The coverage tables below map all **67 topic groups** to their lesson modules; the expanded notes teach the underlying methods and examples within those groups.

## Scope note

The supplied expanded PDF describes itself as a practical preparation checklist built by expanding broad syllabus headings. It explicitly says its extra subtopics are study guidance, not a guarantee that every item is officially prescribed or will appear. Use the latest NTA CMAT notice for official scope and exam rules. Current affairs, scheme eligibility, office holders and economic figures must be verified close to the exam date.
